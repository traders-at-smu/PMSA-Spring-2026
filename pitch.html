Background

What Is a Prediction Market?

A marketplace where you trade YES/NO contracts on real-world events. If you're right, the contract pays $1.00. If you're wrong, it pays $0. The price reflects the crowd's estimated probability.

What Are We Arbitraging?

Polymarket and Kalshi list the same events but price them differently. We buy YES on one venue and NO on the other. If the combined cost is under $1.00, we profit no matter what happens.

The Math

Formulas

Arbitrage Condition
PYES + PNO < $1.00
Buy YES on venue A + NO on venue B. Total cost under $1 = profit.
Net Profit Per Contract
Net = $1.00 − PYES − PNO − Fees
Payout minus what we paid minus exchange fees.
Fee Formulas
Poly = 0.1% × price
Kalshi = ⌈7% × p × (1 − p)⌉cent
Annualized ROI
ROIann = (1 + Net / Cost) 365 / D − 1
D = days to expiration. Short contracts compound to huge annualized returns.
